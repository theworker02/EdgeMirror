# Commercial licensing — EdgeMirror

EdgeMirror is **source-available proprietary software**, not open source.

- **Evaluation:** You may view, clone, and try EdgeMirror for non-production evaluation under [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE).
- **Everything else:** Production use, redistribution, SaaS/hosted offerings, CI gates for shipping products, and other commercial use require a **paid commercial license**.

This file describes how to inquire about a commercial license. It is **not** legal advice and is **not** itself a binding quote or purchase agreement.

## What a commercial license typically covers

Commercial terms are negotiated case-by-case. Topics often include:

- Production and internal business use
- Redistribution / OEM embedding (if needed)
- Hosted / SaaS / multi-tenant use
- Support or update expectations (if offered)
- Seat, org, or deployment scope
